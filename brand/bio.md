## Long bio (about 150 words)

Artem Mashkov was born in the Soviet Union, turned out Jewish, and became American, so in most movies he's both of the bad guys. His comedy is dark, deadpan and geopolitical: ChatKGB, Russian superstitions, polyamory, and watching America from a country that already tried all of this.

He started stand-up in July 2025 and has done about 100 shows since, at The Stand, New York Comedy Club, Laughing Buddha, Broadway Comedy Club, Grisly Pear, Rodney's and Brooklyn indie rooms, plus English-language rooms in Geneva and Lausanne. He made the final of the 2026 Laughing Buddha Festival, won a Buddha Battle that earned him a residency at The Stand, and has opened for Ahmed Al-Kadri.

He splits his time between New York and Geneva, 45 days at a time, and runs a startup in between, which is its own dark joke.
